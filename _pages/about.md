Hello! I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Psychological & Brain Sciences at Washington University in St. Louis, advised by [Dr. Jeff Zacks](https://dcl.wustl.edu/people/jzacks/) in the [Dynamic Cognition Lab](https://dcl.wustl.edu/). My research focuses on how people perceive and remember the temporal dynamics of everyday events, as well as how this ability changes across the lifespan. To investigate these questions, I use behavioral experiments along with eye-tracking, neuroimaging, and computational modeling approaches. 

Previously I obtained my B.S. in Cognitive Studies from Vanderbilt University in 2022, where I worked with [Dr. Daniel Levin](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/psychological_sciences/bio/daniel-levin) as an honors thesis student. Outside of research, I love reading, singing operatic and music theater pieces, picking up new languages, and experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen :)
